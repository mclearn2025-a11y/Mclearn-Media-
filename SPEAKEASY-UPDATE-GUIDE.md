# Speakeasy — How to Check an Update Is Live

Follow these steps after any change to Speakeasy is sent to GitHub.

## 1. Check the update on GitHub
Go to github.com/mclearn2025-a11y/Mclearn-Media- and sign in. The newest
change is listed near the top. If you see it, the update is saved.

## 2. Open Cloudflare Pages
Go to dash.cloudflare.com and sign in. Click **Workers & Pages** on the left,
then click the McLearn Media project. This is the project that publishes this
repo as the website.

## 3. Confirm the deploy finished
Click the **Deployments** tab. The top entry should be the newest change with a
green **Success** label.
- **Building:** wait a minute or two, then refresh.
- **Failed, or nothing new:** click **Create deployment** (or **Retry
  deployment**) and choose the `main` branch.

## 4. Open Speakeasy on your phone
Go to your website address with `/speakeasy/` at the end. If you still see the
old version, refresh the page. If that doesn't work, close the tab and open it
again so the phone stops using its saved copy.

## 5. Test the keyboard
Tap the **Keyboard** tab. The letter keys should be big and tall and fill most
of the screen. Type a few letters, tap a suggested word, then tap **Speak** and
make sure you hear it out loud. Check that the gear button at the top right
shows fully on the screen.

## 6. Test the old link
Go to your website address with `/speak-easy-2` at the end. It should jump
straight to `/speakeasy/`.

## 7. Put it on the home screen
- **iPhone:** open the page in Safari, tap **Share**, then tap **Add to Home
  Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then tap **Add to Home
  screen** or **Install app**.

Now Speakeasy opens like a regular app, with one tap.
