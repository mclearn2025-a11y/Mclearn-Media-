Story canon

The hundred and nine.

From the novels. Every figure in this room belongs to the book sequence. None of it is McLearn Media casework or verified reporting, and it should not be read as either.

Every name here signed something a long time ago and was never told what it did. The work, in the story, is to walk that back with a paper, read aloud, in front of the person it belongs to.

How a name becomes a line

Step 1

Locate

A name on a page is not yet a person. The story says: drive until you find them.

Step 2

Read

Sit at a table. Read the assignment aloud. Do not soften what the paper says.

Step 3

Notice

The fictional process files a termination and keeps the receipt. Real procedure varies by jurisdiction.

Step 4

Witness

The story's rule is that none of it holds without names on the paper who watched it signed.
